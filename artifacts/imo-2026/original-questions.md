## Day 1 — Problem 1

There are 2026 integers greater than 1 written on a blackboard, not necessarily different. In a move, Confucius chooses two integers `x > 1` and `y > 1` from different places on the blackboard and replaces them with `gcd(x, y)` and `lcm(x, y) / gcd(x, y)`. He continues while this is possible.

1. Prove that, regardless of the choices, after finitely many moves exactly one integer `A` on the blackboard is greater than 1.
2. Prove that the value of `A` does not depend on the choices.

## Day 1 — Problem 2

Let `ABC` be a triangle and let `M` and `N` be the midpoints of `AB` and `AC`. Let `K` and `L` be strictly inside triangles `BMC` and `BNC`, respectively, such that `K` lies strictly inside triangle `ABL` and `L` lies strictly inside triangle `AKC`. Suppose `∠KBA = ∠ACL`, `∠LBK = ∠LNC`, and `∠LCK = ∠BMK`. Let `O` be the circumcentre of `AKL`. Prove `OM = ON`.

## Day 1 — Problem 3

Let `n` be positive. Liu Bang and Xiang Yu have a stick of length 1. Liu marks at most `n` points, then Xiang marks at most `n` distinct further points. The stick is cut at all marked points. They then claim unclaimed pieces alternately, Liu first, each maximising their own total length. For each `n`, determine the largest `c` that Liu can guarantee, regardless of Xiang's play.

## Day 2 — Problem 4

Let `0° < θ < 180°`. Shan-Yu initially chooses a paper triangle `T`. If `T` has an angle `θ`, Mulan wins. Otherwise she chooses a nonvertex perimeter point, cuts it to its opposite vertex, and Shan-Yu discards one resulting triangle; the other becomes `T`. Determine the real `θ` for which Mulan can force a win in finitely many steps, no matter how Shan-Yu plays.

## Day 2 — Problem 5

Let `R_{>0}` be the positive reals. Determine all `f : R_{>0} → R_{>0}` such that, for every `x, y ∈ R_{>0}`, `sqrt((x^2 + f(y)^2) / 2) ≥ (f(x) + y) / 2 ≥ sqrt(x f(y))`.

## Day 2 — Problem 6

Let `a₁, a₂, a₃, …` be positive integers greater than 1. For every positive `n`, `a_{n+1}` is the smallest positive integer greater than `a_n` such that `gcd(a_{n+1}, a_i) > 1` for every `i = 1, 2, …, n`. Prove that positive `T, L` exist with `a_{n+T} = a_n + L` for every positive `n`.
